Quick method

Remember this

1
Rectangle

Area = length × breadth

2
Square

Area = side × side

3
Remaining area

Total area − covered area

Study tip

Always write square units

Perimeter uses cm or m. Area uses cm² or m². Check the units before submitting an answer.
